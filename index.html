ℹ️ ABOUT ARROW POP

Master Archer • Balloon Blaster Deluxe

🎯 How To Play

Click/touch and drag backwards from the bow to aim and adjust pull tension. Release to fire your arrow. Pop all targets before running out of arrows!

🎈 Special Balloons

💣 Bomb: Explosive chain reaction pops nearby balloons.
❄️ Freeze: Stasis slows ascending balloons for 5s.
🏹 Quiver: Restores +3 arrows to your quiver.
⭐ Star: Grants bonus points and extra coins.

🔥 Fire Rush & Armory

Maintain a 4x combo to ignite Fire Rush for flaming piercing arrows. Earn coins to unlock elemental bows in the Armory!
